INNOVATION · NO RETRAINING

New hazards from a word, not a dataset

Say the object, SayGuard finds it

YOLO-World v2-S detects whatever the rule names — excavator, forklift, ladder. It runs only on cameras that have such a rule, every third frame: 12 ms on the PC, 46.6 FPS on Hailo-10H.

Distance from head size

A worker’s head is about 0.23 m tall, which gives metres per pixel right next to them. “Within 3 metres” needs no camera calibration.

Rule spoken in Mandarin → live alert: “excavator 0.5 m”

SayGuard · Stage I proposal · 8 · demo footage: Mixkit free license

Our footage had no forklifts, so we said a rule about an excavator instead. The open-vocabulary detector found it from the word alone, and the alert fired with an estimated distance of half a metre. That is the point: a new hazard is a sentence, not a data-collection project.

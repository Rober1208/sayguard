These are six test buildings the detector never trained on. Warehouse_010 is our demo scene, so it keeps the demo zones and the tracker was developed while watching it; in the other five, AI agents drew the zones from coverage maps before any result existed. Covered means the event got its own alert or fell inside the cooldown of an alert for that worker, and the own-alert column is the strict count; the forklift rule is low there by design, because its cooldown does not re-alert a worker stepping in and out of range of a parked forklift. The headline numbers come after two alert-logic fixes made on non-test scenes once we had seen the frozen run, shown on the right in grey, and false alerts rose in aisles and work areas with those fixes. On the far right, fusing all cameras on one floor plan keeps the head count close to the truth, while summing the cameras overcounts.

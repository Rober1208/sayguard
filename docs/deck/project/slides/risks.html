These are the limits we know of today, each with a plan. The biggest is forklifts in unfamiliar buildings: in two of the six test warehouses the detector missed most of them, so we plan a short fine-tune on each customer's own vehicles, tested once on new footage. Nothing has run on the UGen300 yet, so throughput over USB and the load of warehouse mode are the first measurements of Stage II; the warehouse detector keeps its accuracy at the cameras' 16:9 shape, which cuts its work per frame. Hailo's guide says two generative models cannot run at the same time, so speech and the language model run one after the other while the detectors keep going. Spoken Mandarin loses some rules to words Whisper hears differently; we fix those on new sentences and the officer confirms every rule anyway. Worker IDs change too often to show, so the dashboard counts and alerts without them. And because every AI answer is typed and checked, a misread rule or a filtered alert stays visible to the officer instead of failing silently.

TWO MODES TODAY

Hard-hat mode for construction sites

Warehouse mode with zones and forklift distances in metres

SayGuard · Stage I proposal · 3

This is the product. One small box sits next to the site's video recorder and watches every camera at once. The safety officer adds rules by simply saying them, hard-hat alerts are double-checked before anyone is paged, a daily safety report is written automatically, and no video ever leaves the site. Today it runs in two modes: hard-hat mode for construction sites, and warehouse mode, where all cameras are fused into one live floor plan with zones and forklift distances in metres.

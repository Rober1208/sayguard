# Demo video sources

The demo camera streams are cut from free stock clips on [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/), used under the [Mixkit Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree). Each stream is 60 s starting at the listed offset, scaled to 960 px wide, 10 fps, audio removed (clips shorter than 60 s are looped).

The footage itself is not redistributed in this repository; `scripts/fetch_videos.py`, `scripts/score_clips.py` and `scripts/make_streams.py` re-create the streams.

THE PROBLEM

Sites have cameras. Nobody is watching them.

Found too late

A missing hard hat is seen in recorded footage after an incident — if anyone looks at all.

A new hazard needs a new model

AI cameras detect a fixed list. A forklift zone or a ladder rule means collecting data and retraining.

Alarms nobody trusts

False alerts teach people to ignore them, and streaming site video to the cloud raises privacy and bandwidth concerns.

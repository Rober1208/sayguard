We tested what we could on the PC and in Hailo’s emulator. The open risks are about the device itself, including the load of warehouse mode, and about sites that look different from our test scenes. Each one has a measurement planned in Stage II and a fallback that keeps the officer in control; for unfamiliar forklifts the plan is fine-tuning on the customer’s vehicles, and we have not yet measured how much data that needs.

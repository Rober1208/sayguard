Sites already own cameras and are responsible for hard hats and forklift safety; what they rarely have is someone watching every screen. We sell one SayGuard box per site area, a UGen300 with a small host next to the recorder, and CCTV installers put it in alongside the ASUS IoT hardware they already handle; a bigger site simply adds boxes. The accelerator is cheap per camera, draws very little power, and nothing goes to the cloud.

SayGuard · Stage I proposal · 3

This is the whole product in one picture. The site's existing cameras, plus a camera wherever a watched zone is seen by fewer than two, feed one small box next to the recorder, and every model runs locally, on site: on a PC GPU in today's prototype, on a host with an ASUS UGen300 in Stage II. The safety officer gets double-checked alerts, a live floor plan, a daily report, and adds rules by voice. It works in two modes today: hard-hat mode for construction sites, and warehouse mode, where all cameras are fused onto one floor plan.

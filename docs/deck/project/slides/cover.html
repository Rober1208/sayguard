SayGuard is designed to put one ASUS UGen300 next to the video recorder of each site area and watch every camera in it; today the Stage I prototype runs the same software on a local PC GPU. Safety officers set the rules by saying them, in Mandarin or English. Like TypeSafe AI's Jev, every decision SayGuard asks an AI model for comes back as a typed answer rather than text, which keeps the models small enough for a 2.5-watt chip and easy for code to check. Figures in this deck come from our working prototype or from our own INT8 checks in Hailo's emulator, unless they are labelled as a Hailo or ASUS reference or as an estimate.

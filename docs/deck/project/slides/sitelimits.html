Camera coverage is the clearest difference we see between these two scenes, though not a proven cause. On the left, Warehouse_010: almost every part of the floor is seen by at least two cameras, and the forklift rule covers nearly every event with no false alerts. On the right, Warehouse_020, our validation scene: sixteen cameras in one large hall leave gaps, it also has about twice the people and is the same building as the training scenes, and the same rule degrades. The detector also misses most forklifts in two of the test buildings for reasons we have not established yet; the planned mitigation is fine-tuning on the customer’s own vehicles, and how much data that takes is still to be measured. That gives a coverage spec of one UGen300 per area of up to twelve cameras, with every watched zone seen by at least two cameras, and the compute load of warehouse mode on the device is the first thing we measure in Stage II.

This is the real dashboard in hard-hat mode, captured in full HD. Ten looping site videos are analysed live; red boxes mark people without a hard hat, and faces are pixelated locally. Each alert shows whether the double-check confirmed it, and the Say a rule panel is where the officer speaks a new rule. Today it runs on a PC GPU; in Stage II the same software runs on the UGen300.

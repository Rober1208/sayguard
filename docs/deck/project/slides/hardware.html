The hardware stays simple: the cameras a site already has, a small host, and one UGen300 per area. Today the same software runs on a PC GPU, and in hard-hat mode ten cameras through one batched detector would use an estimated forty percent of one UGen300. In Stage II every neural network moves to the accelerator, while the host decodes video, places workers on the floor plan, applies the rules and serves the dashboard. The twelve-camera limit comes from camera coverage in our test scenes; the load of warehouse mode on the device is not measured yet, and it is the first thing we measure in Stage II.

THE PROBLEM

Sites have cameras. Few have anyone watching them live.

Found too late

A missing hard hat may only be noticed in recorded footage, after an incident.

Rules are fixed

Many AI cameras ship with a set list of rules. A new zone, distance, head-count or time rule means reconfiguring by hand.

Alarms get ignored

Frequent false alerts wear down trust, and streaming site video to the cloud raises privacy and bandwidth concerns.

SayGuard · Stage I proposal · 2

Construction sites and warehouses already have cameras, but few have anyone watching them live, so a missing hard hat may only be noticed in recorded footage after an incident. Many AI cameras ship with a set list of rules, so a new zone, distance, head-count or time rule means reconfiguring by hand. And alarms that are often wrong tend to be ignored, while streaming site video to the cloud raises privacy and bandwidth concerns.

This is what SayGuard promises a site, and how we will know it holds. For the site: every camera watched, one double-checked alert per worker, rules added by voice and confirmed by the officer, a daily report nobody has to rewrite, and no video leaving the site. What we measured in Stage I: the alert check is right 95 percent of the time in 15 milliseconds; unseen rules come out exactly right 95 and 84 to 88 percent of the time when typed, and 90 and 74 to 86 percent when spoken by synthetic voices over site noise; the report never aims an action at the wrong problem; the warehouse head count stays within two people; and in Hailo's emulator the INT8 detectors nearly match their float versions. Stage II succeeds when one UGen300 analyses every frame of ten cameras, when the chip's accuracy matches the emulator, when the rule tests pass on the Hailo language-model build, when we know how many warehouse cameras one box takes, and when alert latency and power are measured end to end.

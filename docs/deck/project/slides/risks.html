SayGuard · Stage I proposal · 17 · docs/results/

These are the limits we know of today, each with a plan. The biggest is forklifts in unfamiliar buildings: in two of the six test warehouses the detector missed most of them, so we plan a short fine-tune on each customer's own vehicles. Worker IDs change too often to show, so the dashboard counts and alerts without them. Large halls need the coverage spec. Nothing has run on the UGen300 yet, so throughput over USB and the load of warehouse mode are the first measurements of Stage II. And because every AI answer is typed and checked, a misread rule or a filtered alert stays visible to the officer instead of failing silently.
